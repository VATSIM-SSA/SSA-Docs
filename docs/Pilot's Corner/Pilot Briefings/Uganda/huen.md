# Entebbe International (HUEN)

*Pilots taking part in events that depart from or arrive at HUEN should make themselves familiar with this briefing page as well as with all existing charts.*

## Overview

- Entebbe International Airport is the principal international airport of Uganda.

- The airport has runway 17/35.

## Charts

- HUEN does not have any charts publicly available.

## Departure Briefing

### Clearance Delivery

- HUEN does not have a real world delivery position.
  **HUEN_GND** will be the position responsible for providing you with a departure clearance.

- Stand Assignments: Due to limited stand capacity at HUEN, we kindly ask that you use the following stands based off the type of flight you are doing:
    - **Passenger:** Main Terminal
    - **Cargo:** Apron 2 / VIP Terminal
    - **Private:** Apron 3 / Apron 4
    - **Jumbo Aircraft (all types):** Apron 1
    - *(Note: Arriving aircraft may also be subject to stand changes issued by the GND controller).*

- When requesting IFR clearance, please provide your **Aircraft Type**, **Stand Number** and **ATIS letter.**

- For Cross Africa flights, pilots may be given a cruise flight Level that allows for en-route separation.
  It might not match your requested flight level.

### Departure Procedures

- Pilots should expect an **APNAD** departure out of Entebbe for both runways.

- Comply with your assigned departure procedure and do not climb above your initial climb altitude unless instructed so.

- You may be given intermediate climb instructions due to traffic.
  Do not climb above any assigned flight level.

- Comply with any speed restrictions if provided.
  They are important to maintain adequate separation between traffic.

- When approaching the runway, you'll be asked to report fully ready for departure.
  That means that when given line-up or take-off clearance, you're expected to be ready to do so without delay.
  If you need more time, please advise the tower.

### Ground Operations

- **HUEN_GND / TWR** will be the position responsible for providing push-back and taxiing instructions.

- Remember to squawk **Mode C** before taxiing.

- If told to monitor a frequency, you're expected to tune in the frequency but not to contact the controller.
  They will contact you when able.

### Code F Restrictions

- Taxiing for Code F aircraft should be conducted with caution.
  Ensure you park on **Apron 1**.

### Departure Timeline

- For Cross Africa flights, your slot time is represented as a **CTOT (Calculated Take off Time).**
  You'll be expected to push-back at **TOBT (Target Off Block Time)** between 10 and 15 minutes before **CTOT (depending on how the traffic is flowing at that time).**

- As far as clearance is concerned, you can request it at any time before **TOBT**, as long as you'll be ready for push-back at **TOBT.**
  We suggest you request your clearance at least 15 minutes before **TOBT.**

- You should not need to connect to the network more than 60 minutes before your **CTOT.**

## Arrival Briefing

### Arrivals and Approaches

- As your flight is approaching HUEN, please make yourself familiar with the arrival and approach procedures for Entebbe.

- All pilots expect Runway 17 or Runway 35 for landing, depending on wind conditions.

- Pilots should plan for an RNAV STAR (such as the APNAD, ALKON, or LABAT arrivals) and the **ILS / RNAV** for the active runway. 

- On initial contact with Entebbe Approach or Entebbe Control, you will be told the landing runway and the arrival.

### Parking and Ground Operations

- When landing, pilots are advised to vacate as quickly as possible to allow for smooth operation.
