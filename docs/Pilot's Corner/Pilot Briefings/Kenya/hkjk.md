# Jomo Kenyatta International (HKJK)

*Pilots taking part in events that depart from or arrive at HKJK should make themselves familiar with this briefing page as well as with all existing charts.*

## Overview

- Jomo Kenyatta International Airport is an international airport in Nairobi, the capital of and largest city in Kenya.

- The airport has runway 06/24.
  Runway 06 is the preferred runway up to a tailwind component of 10-15 knots.

- A one-way system operates throughout Apron 1:
    - **06 in use:** Taxi out via J or K. Taxi in via M or H.
    - **24 in use:** Taxi out via M or H. Taxi in via J or K.

## Departure Briefing

### Clearance Delivery

- HKJK does not have a real world delivery position.
  **HKJK_GND** is responsible for giving clearance.

- Stand Assignments: If you don't know which stand to use for your airline, we recommend spawning on a "J" stand.
  Freighters should use Apron 2 "F" stands.

- When requesting IFR clearance, please provide your **Aircraft Type**, **Stand Number** and **ATIS letter.**

- Initial climb is not assigned on a per-SID basis; it depends on your direction of flight.
  During clearance, make sure to readback your SID, transition (if applicable), initial climb, and squawk.

### Departure Procedures

- Comply with your assigned departure procedure.
  If Nairobi Radar issues the instruction "climb now", all SID altitude restrictions may be disregarded.
  Otherwise, all climb instructions must be treated with regard to the SID restrictions.

- You may be given intermediate climb instructions due to traffic.
  Do not climb above any assigned flight level.

- Comply with any speed restrictions if provided.
  They are important to maintain adequate separation between traffic.

- When approaching the runway, you'll be asked to report fully ready for departure.
  That means that when given line-up or take-off clearance, you're expected to be ready to do so without delay.
  If you need more time, please advise the tower.

### Ground Operations

- **HKJK_GND / TWR** will be the position responsible for providing push-back and taxiing instructions.

- Expect a few minutes delay for pushback during busy events.
  To avoid delays, only ask for push once the pushback tug is fully connected.

- Remember to squawk **Mode C** before taxiing.

- **Taxiway G Shortcut:** On Taxiway G, there are two taxi lines which act as a shortcut onto K for Runway 06.
  Do not use this shortcut unless instructed by ATC.

### Code F Restrictions

- Taxiing for Code F aircraft should be conducted with caution.
  Consult charts for specific taxiway width restrictions.

### Departure Timeline

- For Cross Africa, your slot time is represented as a **CTOT (Calculated Take off Time).**
  You'll be expected to push-back at **TOBT (Target Off Block Time)** between 15 and 20 minutes before **CTOT (depending on how the traffic is flowing at that time).**

- As far as clearance is concerned, you can request it at any time before **TOBT**, as long as you'll be ready for push-back at **TOBT.**
  We suggest you request your clearance at least 15 minutes before **TOBT.**

- You should not need to connect to the network more than 60 minutes before your **CTOT.**

## Arrival Briefing

### Arrivals and Approaches

- As your flight is approaching HKJK, please make yourself familiar with the arrival and approach procedures for Nairobi.

- When Runway 06 is in use, expect the ILS.
  When Runway 24 is in use, expect an RNP approach.

- STAR Planning: Most STARs have multiple variations. 
    - For **Runway 06**, program a **"Papa"** arrival (e.g., NAKAT2P) with a JOMOK ILS transition for planning purposes, unless instructed otherwise. 
    - For **Runway 24**, program a **"Romeo"** arrival (e.g., KIMET1R).

- Due to being a single runway airport, expect to enter published holds during busy events.

- On initial contact with Nairobi Radar or Nairobi Control, you will be told the landing runway and the arrival. 

- When cleared for the ILS 06 via a JOMOK2x transition, descend to the platform altitude of 7,100ft to establish, unless radar vectors are provided.

### Parking and Ground Operations

- When landing, pilots are advised to vacate as quickly as possible to allow for smooth operation. 
  - **Runway 06:** Medium aircraft plan to vacate via E or L. Heavy aircraft plan to vacate via L.
  - If vacating via L, continue taxi via G and await further instructions. If vacating via any other taxiway, hold short of G.
- Tower will issue taxi instructions to the start of the Apron before handing you off to Ground for stand allocation.
